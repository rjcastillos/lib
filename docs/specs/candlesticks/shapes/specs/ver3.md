3.	Entry signal
	a.	Long
		i. last 2 candles open and close are above EMA9 and open < close (green)
	b. Short
		i. last 2 candles open and close are below EMA9 and open > open (red) 
4. Close position 
	a. Long	
		i.   If last 2 candles open and close are below EMA9
		ii.  Price below ATRPx1.5
		iii. If when the candle opens the price is 5% above entry price and last candle close is below EMA9
		iv.  if when the candle opens the price is below EMA9 and the last 2 candles open > close (red)
		v.   if by one minute before the current candle closes is lower then last candle and 3 out of the last 4 candles are reaching lower lows
	b. Short
		i. if last 2 candles open and close are above EMA9 
			Price Above ATRPx1.5
		ii.  If when the candle opens the price is 3% below entry price
