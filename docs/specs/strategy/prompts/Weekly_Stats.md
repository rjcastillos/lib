## Make a script to calculate weekly statistics for any asset choosen in the chart

## apply the dates filter to the current asset in the chart and calculate the following statistics:

- Percentage of Mondays where the close price is greater than the open price , to this condition we will call it Positive Mondays.

- Percentage of weeks with Positive Mondays and the week close price is greater than the previous week close price.

- Percentage of Tuesdays where the close price is greater than the open price and are after a Positive Monday , to this condition we will call it Positive Tuesdays after Positive Mondays.

- Percentage of weeks with Positive Tuesdays and Positive Mondays and the week close price is greater than the previous week close price.

- When Monday or Tuesday is a holiday do not count that week in the statistics.

- Percentage of weeks with all the days of the week with bullish candles, to this condition we will call it Positive Weekstreak

- Percentage of weeks where the weekly close price is greater than the previous week close price, to this condition we will call it Positive weeks. ^

- Percentage of Mondays where the close price is less than the Monday open price , to this condition we will call it Negative Mondays.

- Percentage of Positve Tuesdays after Negative Mondays, to this condition we will call it Positive Tuesdays after Negative Mondays.

- Percentage of weeks with Positive Tuesdays and after a Negative Monday and the week close price is greater than the previous week close price.

- Percentage of weeks with Negative Mondays and the week close price is greater than the previous week close price.

- Percentage of holiday weeks  (Either Monday or Tuesday is a holiday)

- Percentage of holiday weeks with last day of that week where close is greater than the previous week close price.
